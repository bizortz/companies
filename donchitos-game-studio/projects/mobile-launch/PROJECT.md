Take a title that has cleared production (per `new-game-kickoff` and the studio's own production pipeline) through the full mobile launch sequence: compliance and monetization setup, store listing optimization, soft-launch submission, soft-launch execution and KPI evaluation, a CEO go/no-go ruling for global launch, UA scale-up, and the handoff into a recurring live-ops cadence. This project is the mobile-specific counterpart to `new-game-kickoff` — it owns everything between "the game is built" and "the game is live, scaling, and in steady-state live-ops," across the Publishing & Growth pillar plus the CEO's global-launch authority.

## Milestones

1. **Compliant & Monetized** — Privacy/compliance artifacts and monetization catalog complete
2. **Listing Ready** — ASO listing package complete and store submission accepted for soft launch
3. **Soft-Launch Verdict** — Soft launch run to completion with a PASS/EXTEND/KILL-RESHAPE verdict
4. **Global Launch Decision** — CEO go/no-go ruling issued
5. **Scaling & Live** — UA scaled per the go decision, live-ops cadence established

## Task Sequence (chained in this order)

1. `privacy-compliance` (legal-compliance-officer)
2. `monetization-setup` (monetization-designer)
3. `aso-update` (aso-specialist)
4. `store-submission-soft-launch` (release-manager)
5. `soft-launch` (publishing-director)
6. `kpi-review` (analytics-engineer)
7. `go-no-go-global-launch` (ceo)
8. `ua-campaign-scale-up` (ua-manager)
9. `live-ops-cadence` (live-ops-designer)
