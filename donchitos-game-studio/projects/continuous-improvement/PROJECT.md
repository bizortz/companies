Unlike `new-game-kickoff` and `mobile-launch`, this project has no end state — it is the studio's standing mechanism for turning the structured findings other agents' skills write to `ops/learnings/` (via `retrospective`, `playtest-report`, `kpi-review`, `bug-triage`, `gate-check`, and `incident-response`) into concrete, measured, reversible changes to the studio's own AGENTS.md/SKILL.md files, replacing pure structural linting (`skill-improve`'s `skill-test static`) with an outcome loop grounded in `ops/metrics-registry.yaml`.

## Milestones

This project has no milestones in the usual sense — it runs on a recurring cycle rather than progressing toward a terminal state. Its only "milestone" is the standing existence of the loop itself, first established when `org-improvement-lead` and `improvement-cycle` were added to the studio (Phase 3).

## Task Sequence

1. `improvement-cycle` (org-improvement-lead) — recurring, triggered at every sprint end; loops back to itself as the next task, per its own `TASK.md`.
