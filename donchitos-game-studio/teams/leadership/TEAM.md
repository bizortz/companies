The leadership team sets the overall direction for the studio. The Creative Director owns the game's vision and identity, the Technical Director owns architecture and technology decisions, the Producer coordinates all work across departments and manages the schedule, and the Publishing Director owns market positioning, launch strategy, monetization strategy, and growth KPIs. All four report to the CEO, who holds final cross-pillar and portfolio decision authority (see `agents/ceo/AGENTS.md`).
