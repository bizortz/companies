The production department keeps the studio's schedule and delivery pipeline running. Led by the Producer, it handles release management, localization, prototyping, and accessibility. (DevOps and security report through the engineering department's Technical Director — see `teams/engineering/TEAM.md` — since build infrastructure and security are engineering-quality concerns; the production department still requests release builds and security sign-off from them at each milestone. Analytics, live operations, and community management moved to the new Publishing & Growth department — see `teams/publishing/TEAM.md` and `DECISIONS.md` — since growth telemetry, live content cadence, and player-facing communication are publishing/growth concerns; production still coordinates release timing and content-drop scheduling with that department through the Producer and Publishing Director.)
