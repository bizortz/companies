The Publishing & Growth department is the fourth pillar of Donchitos Game Studio, alongside Creative, Engineering, and Production, and reports directly to the CEO through the Publishing Director. It owns everything between "the game exists" and "the game is discovered, retained, monetized fairly, and supported" — market positioning and launch strategy, paid user acquisition and ASO, IAP/ad monetization design, market and competitive research, growth analytics, live operations, community management, and first-line player support.

This department was created by moving `analytics-engineer`, `community-manager`, and `live-ops-designer` here from the Production department, since growth telemetry, live content cadence, and player-facing communication are publishing/growth concerns rather than production-schedule concerns — see `DECISIONS.md` for the record of that move. `community-manager` in turn manages `player-support`, the studio's first support hire. `economy-designer` remains under Creative (via `game-designer`) since in-game resource-economy design is a game-design discipline; `monetization-designer` here owns the real-money and ad-revenue surface and coordinates closely with `economy-designer` on anything touching the shared currency model.

`legal-compliance-officer` is **not** part of this department despite working on regulatory questions (privacy, age ratings, COPPA/GDPR, ATT, Play Data Safety, loot-box regulation) that directly affect publishing and monetization decisions. It reports directly to the CEO instead — both to stay under the studio's max-7-direct-reports cap (this department's manager, the Publishing Director, was already at 8 direct reports before the fix) and, more importantly, to keep compliance review independent of the pillar whose growth and monetization incentives it is meant to check. See `DECISIONS.md` for the full rationale.
